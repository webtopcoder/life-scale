# Grander Landing Page Hero Subline

## Goal
Rewrite the hero subtitle to make IQ Scale feel more distinctive and aspirational — built around the user's own life, not external expectations. Keep it concise, readable, and original.

## Sections to update
1. **Hero subtitle** (lines 116-119 in `src/pages/LandingPage.tsx`)

## What we will not touch
- Hero headline: keeps "We give you the IQ of You."
- Method section, post-test gains section, categories section, pricing, FAQ, and "What you get" sections.
- Existing design tokens and layout.

## Proposed copy

### Hero subtitle
From:
> IQ Scale puts a number and a plan on any part of your life. Mind and Body are live now, each with three short tests. Money, People, and Work are coming.

To:
> A score for the life you actually lead — not the one the world expects. Mind and Body are live now, each with three short assessments. Money, People, and Work are next.

Rationale: The subline reframes the product as a personal scorecard rather than a borrowed standard. It captures the "internally developed, anchored to you" positioning without repeating the same words back to the reader.

- Run the local dev build and visually check the hero on mobile and desktop.
- Confirm the subtitle does not break the two-column layout or wrap awkwardly.
- Keep the plain-language constraint intact; no jargon or inflated claims.
